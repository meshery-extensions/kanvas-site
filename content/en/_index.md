# Kanvas

Kanvas is a visual and collaborative experience for designing and operating cloud native infrastructure.

Kanvas diagrams are not just pictures of infrastructure. A Kanvas diagram is a deployable design: import a Helm chart or Kubernetes manifest, review it with your team, and deploy it from the canvas.

## Why Kanvas?
- Visualize infrastructure
- Collaborate with teams
- Troubleshoot with Operator mode tools
